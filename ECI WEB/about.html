WHO WE ARE

Our School

Educare Learning School is a family-owned private school located along Quirino Highway (Naguillan Road), at Purok 4, Irisan, Baguio City. It is situated in the biggest barangay (in terms of land area), of Baguio City, and is six kilometers away from the city’s central business district. Educare started to operate in 2004 for Preschool and Elementary levels. After two years, it was granted a permit to offer Secondary level. In 2007, it was given recognition by the Department of Education for complete curriculum levels of basic education. In its first year of operation for Preschool and Elementary levels, it had three hundred ten (310) students with ten (10) teachers. In its eight years of operation, its population fluctuated from as low as two hundred sixty (260) to its highest number of three hundred sixty-five (365) for the Academic Year 2013-2014.


It had an initial capitalization of around twenty million pesos (Php 20,000,000).


The family thought of putting up the school, expecting its feasibility and advantage because of the distance of our place from the city proper where quality private schools are located. Considering transport cost and time to be spent in attending school in the city proper, a quality private school in the area is both convenient and economical for students and their parents residing within Irisan barangay.


Another consideration for establishing the school is the fast pace of development in the area. Numerous subdivisions and businesses are being established to serve the needs of its growing population. The expectation of management is that the school will grow with the community.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Philosophy, Mission,Vision and Core Values

P

Philosophy

At Educare, we believe in cultivating a truly special place where people and ideas flourish. Educare is committed to nurture well-rounded individuals whose unique knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values correspond to the needs of our fast-changing world.

M

Mission

As a school encompassing Kindergarten, Elementary, and High School,

Integrated

We committed to preparing students for global citizenship through the seamless adaptation of technology and essential 21st Century Skills;

Empowered Learners

We ensure that every Educare graduate is not only academically proficient but also equipped with the character, critical thinking and adaptability needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world;

Affordable Education

We aspire to provide affordable, and quality education that deeply integrates values formation;

Community Engagement

We Practice community engagement and social responsibility;

Human Resource

We aim to provide good working conditions, performance-based compensation, opportunity for growth, security of tenure, and superior leadership to all our staff.

V

Vision

We envision Educare to be a beacon of educational excellence and a builder of innovative thinkers, ethical leaders and engage global citizens.

CV

Core Values

C – Compassion

Our stakeholders consider the well-being of others by demonstrating kindness, empathy, understanding and inclusivity.


A – Analytical

Our stakeholders demonstrate critical thinking in addressing issues and in solving problems by being rational, organizes and just.


R – Responsible

Our stakeholders consistently manifest genuine integrity, trustworthiness and accountability in the ownership and completion of assigned tasks.


E – Excellence

Our stakeholders exhibit outstanding performance, innovation, and creativity for positive results and continued growth.


S – Stewardship

Our stakeholders show responsible, wise and ethical use of available resources ensuring sustainability.
